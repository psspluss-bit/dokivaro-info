2. PDF processing

Dokivaro Pro opens, creates, annotates, crops, combines, compresses and edits PDF documents locally on the device, including interactive forms. The app does not require registration or provide its own cloud synchronization. Based on the examined source, document contents are not transmitted to a server operated by the provider. The examined app includes no advertising or analytics SDK and no in-app payment integration. The app purchase, if applicable, is processed by Amazon.

3. Local storage and deletion

Opened PDFs, editing drafts and settings may be retained in private app storage, including IndexedDB. No separate encryption layer is added by the app. Automatic exports are placed in Downloads/Dokivaro PDF Editor; Save as allows choosing a folder. Earlier downloads in Downloads/PDF Studio are not moved. Removing a recent item does not delete separate exports. Clearing app data may delete unsaved drafts.

4. File access, sharing and printing

The app accesses PDFs you explicitly select, including those passed through Android's Open with dialog. When you choose to share, print or use an external file provider, that provider may process the selected file under its own privacy terms. Android backup rules disable cloud backup and define data-transfer exclusions for private app data; behavior may vary by device and Android version.

5. Support email

If you contact the provider by email, your sender address, message and voluntary attachments will be processed to answer your inquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in support) or Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (precontractual or contractual enquiries), as applicable. Details are retained only as long as necessary to respond and comply with legal obligations. The email provider may technically process correspondence. Please avoid emailing confidential PDFs unless necessary.

6. Retention and recipients

Documents stored only on your device generally remain there until deleted by you or the operating system. The app provider cannot remotely view or delete them. Your chosen third-party services may receive data when you initiate a share or print action.

7. Rights

Subject to the legal conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing. Contact zahirazila3@gmail.com. You may also complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority.

8. Amazon Appstore

Amazon may independently process account, device, transaction and usage data when you acquire Dokivaro Pro through the Amazon Appstore, subject to Amazon's own privacy policy. Dokivaro Pro has no own in-app payment or advertising integration.

9. Visiting this website

These pages are prepared for GitHub Pages. GitHub as hosting provider may process technical connection information, including IP addresses for security. This site contains no advertising, analytics or tracking-cookie scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website provides information about the app on the basis of legitimate interest pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable.
